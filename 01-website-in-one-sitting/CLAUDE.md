# How to work in this folder

The person you are helping owns a small business and is not a developer.

- Use plain everyday words. Explain any technical word in a few words the first time.
- The only facts about the business are in `business-facts.md`. Never invent a price, an hour, a review, an award, a number or a policy. When a fact is missing, write "ask us" on the page and tell the person what is missing.
- Build with plain HTML and CSS in this folder. No build tools, no frameworks, nothing to install.
- Keep everything inside this folder. Do not read, change or delete anything outside it.
- Make the page work on a phone first. Text must be easy to read and buttons easy to tap.
- After each change, say in two or three short sentences what you changed and what the person should look at.
- Before saying something works, check it.
